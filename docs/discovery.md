## 1. Problem Statement

Women aged 25–50 in Europe regularly buy clothes but fail to build a wardrobe. They spend money impulsively, driven by trends on Instagram and Pinterest, without understanding how pieces work together.

Professional styling help is perceived as expensive and inaccessible. Free fashion content is surface-level and not adapted to real budgets or lifestyles.

**The result:** closets are full, money is wasted, and confidence in personal style does not grow.

> **Core insight:** The problem is not lack of inspiration — it's lack of practical, affordable, trusted guidance.

## 3. Value Proposition

### One-liner
> **Shopping has never been so easy — ready-made capsules and picks for everyday inspiration.**

### Differentiation

| What | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| English language + European context | No direct equivalent exists |
| Real links across mass, mid & luxury tiers | Not just inspiration — actionable |
| "Want It / Can Afford It" rubric | Closes the gap between aspiration and budget |
| AI bot as infrastructure | Scales without proportional manual effort |
| Closed club membership | Increases perceived value of paid subscription |
| Capsules for real life | Vacation, office, season — not abstract runway |

## 4. Lean Canvas

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Women buy chaotically, spend too much, wardrobes don't work. Good content is expensive or shallow. |
| **Customer Segment** | Women 25–50, Europe, middle income, English-speaking or bilingual, Instagram/Pinterest users |
| **Unique Value Proposition** | Personal style editor in Telegram — capsules, picks, real links. Affordable, practical, in English. |
| **Solution** | Open Telegram channel (free content) + Closed club (subscription) + AI bot (onboarding, payment, guide delivery) |
| **Channels** | Instagram, Pinterest (acquisition) → Telegram (monetisation) |
| **Key Metrics** | Open channel subscribers, free→paid conversion, retention at 30/60/90 days, MRR |
| **Unfair Advantage** | English + European market + AI infrastructure + trusted personal editorial voice |
| **Cost Structure** | Content creation (time), AI bot (build/service), paid distribution, co-founder |
| **Revenue Streams** | Base subscription €9–15/mo · Premium with consultation €29–49/mo · One-off guides |

## 5. North Star & Success Metrics

### North Star Metric
> **Number of active paid subscribers who renew after month 1**

This is the single metric that confirms: the product is needed, the content works, people come back.

### Metrics by Stage

| Stage | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-launch** | Waitlist / presale signups | 50–100 before launch |
| **Month 1–3** | Paid subscribers | 50 paying users |
| **Month 3–6** | Retention M1→M2 | >60% |
| **Month 6+** | MRR | €500–1,500 |
| **Scale** | Free→paid conversion rate | >5% |
